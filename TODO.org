Target 2: The Descriptor Set Massacre to fully eliminate UBOs and delete hundreds of lines of C code!

The Benefit for YOU:

    Simplified GPU Culling: In your cull.comp shader, you are currently writing out VkDrawIndexedIndirectCommand. With PVP, you can stop storing vertexOffset entirely. The shader just needs firstIndex and the base address of the vertex buffer (which you already pass as meshSSBOAddr via push constants!).

    Attribute Compression: Your Vertex struct (shown in fill3DVertexInput) uses 64 bytes per vertex (pos=12, color=16, normal=12, uv=8, tangent=16).
        With PVP, you can switch to f16vec2 for UVs and u8vec4 for Normals/Tangents.
        Result: You can likely fit a vertex in 32 bytes (down from 64).
        Impact: That megaVertexBuffer (e.g., 1GB) becomes 500MB. Memory bandwidth (often the main limiter in forward+ PBR) is cut in half.

- Meshlet culling in the cull shader
cull.comp operates per-mesh AABB. Splitting meshes into meshlets (64-triangle clusters) and culling per-meshlet gives you ~4-8x more culling granularity. Combined with VK_EXT_mesh_shader this is the path to truly AAA draw call budgets.
